Privacy Policy

Language Translator: Documents

Last updated 3 November 2026

At Sharkstack Developments we accept that privacy is important to all users. This policy applies to Language Translator: Documents for Android (the "App" or "Service") and informs users of our policies on the collection, use, and disclosure of information when they use it. For this App, it applies in place of our general privacy policy. The App is free to download.

The App has no user accounts. If you use our Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. The information that we collect is used for providing and improving the Service. Our Subscription Terms apply to any paid plan.

Information Collection and Use

We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other registration details to use the App. Some of the information below is processed on your device, and some is sent to the services named in this policy. We use it to provide translations, send notifications, and understand trends in how the App is used.

Translation and Your Text

Online translation. When you have not downloaded the languages for a translation, the text you translate is sent over an encrypted connection to our translation service, hosted on Supabase, along with the language pair and a random ID the App creates for your installation. Our service forwards the text and languages to Google Cloud Translation and returns the result. This applies to typed text and to text recognised from your voice or from a photo. Our service does not store your text, and does not pass your installation ID to Google.

We store the installation ID with the date and the number of characters translated, to enforce usage limits. It does not identify you by name, and clearing the App's data creates a new one. Please avoid translating sensitive information online. Google's handling of this text is described in its Cloud Translation data usage information.

On-device translation. If you download both languages for a translation, it is performed on your device using Google ML Kit, and your text is not sent to our translation service. Language detection and text recognition from images also run on your device.

  • Photos. A photo you take or choose is used on your device to recognise its text, and is not saved or uploaded by the App. Only the recognised text is used for translation.
  • Voice. The App does not save or upload your recordings. Speech is recognised by your device's speech recognition service. The App asks for on-device recognition, but if your device cannot provide it, that service may send your audio to its provider over the internet under its own privacy policy.
  • Read aloud and spell check. These use the speech and spell check services set up on your device, which may have their own online features and privacy policies.
  • Dictionary. A word you look up is sent, with your IP address as part of the connection, to Wiktionary, run by the Wikimedia Foundation. See the Wikimedia privacy policy.
  • Clipboard. The App reads your clipboard only when you choose to paste, and writes to it when you copy.
  • Country. The App reads your SIM or mobile network country, or your time zone, on your device to suggest a translation language. It does not request your location.

On your device. Your settings, downloaded languages, favourites, and history are stored in the App's private storage. History holds the original text and its translation for text and image translations. It keeps your 20 newest records for up to 30 days. Favourites are kept until you remove them.

Permissions

  • Camera. Used to take a photo to translate. If you refuse, you can still choose an image or use the App's other features.
  • Microphone. Used for voice and conversation translation, when you start them. If you refuse, those features are unavailable.
  • Photos. The system picker gives the App only the image you select.
  • Notifications. Used to show notifications on Android 13 and newer. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.
  • Internet and network state. Used for online translation, dictionary lookups, language downloads, analytics, crash reports, remote settings, and notifications.

The App does not request your precise location or contacts. This version does not show ads or use your Advertising ID. We will update this policy before that changes.

Information we obtain from third-party sources

We may be provided with information about the campaign or source an install of the App originated from. Where you buy a paid plan, Google Play tells the App what you have purchased. The App does not offer sign-in through social media accounts.

Log Data

Our App collects analytics data, through Google Analytics for Firebase, which we use to improve the App. The data contains contextual information about users, for example, coarse level location (country, city) derived from your IP address, device model, Android version, App version, language, app instance identifiers, and the dates and times of your use.

We also record screens viewed and, for each translation, its mode (such as text or voice), the languages used, how long it took, whether it succeeded, and the general reason if it failed. These records do not include the text you translate.

We want to inform you that in a case of an error in the App, we collect data and information through Firebase Crashlytics. This may include your device model, operating system version, the App's state at the time of the error, an installation identifier, and the time of the crash. It is not intended to include the text you translate.

The App does not currently have a setting to turn analytics and crash reports off. To stop this collection, you can stop using the App and uninstall it, or contact us with any questions.

Google ML Kit may send Google diagnostic and usage information, such as device and App information, performance metrics, error codes, and a per-installation identifier, and downloads language models from Google. Google describes this in its ML Kit data disclosure.

Notifications and Remote Settings

The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications, which creates a registration token for your installation of the App. It uses Firebase Remote Config to fetch settings that adjust App behaviour, which involves a Firebase installation identifier. Neither includes the text you translate.

Subscriptions

Where a paid plan is offered, purchases and billing are handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Google's privacy policy applies to your payment information.

If you send feedback or contact us, the App opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. We receive what you choose to send, including your email address, your message, and any details or star rating included with it. We use it only to respond and improve the App.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The App does not use browser cookies of its own. However, the third party code and libraries it uses rely on similar technologies, such as app instance identifiers and information stored on your device, for measurement, analytics, and internal operations.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • History is removed automatically as described above. You can also clear it in the App's Settings at any time, and remove favourites individually.
  • Android app backup is turned off for the App, so its data is not included in Android's backups of the App. Uninstalling the App removes its private storage.
  • Your text is not stored by our translation service. Usage counts are kept to operate usage limits, and are not linked to your name.
  • Information collected by Google and other providers is kept according to the settings of those services and their policies.
  • Emails you send us are kept for as long as needed to deal with your request. You can ask us to delete them.
