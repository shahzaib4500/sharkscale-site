Recover Photos: File Recovery

Last updated 3 November 2026

At Sharkstack Developments we accept that privacy is important to all users. This policy applies to Recover Photos: File Recovery for Android (the "App" or "Service") and informs users of our policies on the collection, use, and disclosure of information when they use it. For this App, it applies in place of our general privacy policy. The App is free to download and supported by advertising, with optional paid plans.

The App has no user accounts, and we do not run our own servers for it. If you use our Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. The information that we collect is used for providing and improving the Service. Our Subscription Terms apply to any paid plan.

Information Collection and Use

We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other registration details to use the App. The information described below is processed on your device or collected automatically by the Google services the App uses. We use it to run the App, show advertising, process purchases, and understand trends in how the App is used.

Your Files

Scanning and recovery happen entirely on your device. The App does not upload your photos, videos, audio, documents, file names, or scan results to us or to our advertising and analytics providers.

  • Scanning. The App reads the storage you allow it to access to find files that may be recoverable. Previews are generated on your device and may be cached temporarily in the App's private storage.
  • Recovery. Recovering a file writes a copy to a folder on your device, such as a Restored or PhotoRecovery folder. The App does not change your original files. Because these copies are in shared storage, other apps with storage access, including gallery or backup apps you use, may be able to see them.
  • Settings. Your language, App preferences, and a summary of your last scan (its type, mode, time, and number of files found) are stored on your device.

A file leaves the App only when you choose to send it elsewhere, such as by opening it in another app or attaching an image to feedback. That app or service then handles it under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Photos, videos, and audio. Used to find and preview recoverable media of the types you scan. On Android versions that support it, you can give access to selected photos only. If you refuse, the App cannot scan that media.
  • All files access. Optional and off until you turn it on in your device's system settings. It lets the App scan for documents and look through more of your storage, and write recovered files to its Restored folder. You can turn it off at any time; document scanning then stops working.
  • Storage (older Android versions). Used to read files and, on Android 9 and older, write recovered files.
  • Internet and network state. Used for advertising, analytics, remote settings, purchases, and App updates. Scanning and recovery do not need an internet connection.
  • Advertising ID. Used as described under Advertising.
  • Billing. Used to make purchases through Google Play.

The App does not request your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Information we obtain from third-party sources

Advertising partners. We may be provided with information about which ad network and advertising campaign an install of the App originated from. Google Play also tells the App what you have purchased. The App does not offer sign-in through social media accounts.

Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob, unless you have a plan that removes them. No ad is shown while a scan or recovery is running, or on a file preview. To deliver, limit, and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process your device's Advertising ID, IP address, device and App information, and your interactions with ads. Ads may be personalised where permitted and where you have not opted out. Google's explanation of how it uses information from apps has more detail. Ad providers do not receive your files from the App.

Where required, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App asks for your choices through Google's consent form. You can change them later from the privacy options row in Settings, which appears when it applies to you. On Android you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. If you tap an ad, the advertiser's own privacy policy applies to its site or app.

Log Data

Our App collects analytics data, through Google Analytics for Firebase, which we use to improve the App. The data contains contextual information about users, for example, coarse level location (country, city) derived from your IP address, device model, Android version, App version, language, app instance identifiers, and the dates and times of your use. It also includes session information and purchase events. It does not include your files, file names, or scan results.

The App does not use a separate crash reporting service. If you choose to share diagnostics with Google through your device, Google Play may provide us with general crash and performance statistics.

Remote Settings and App Updates

The App uses Firebase Remote Config to fetch settings that adjust App behaviour, such as how ads and paid plans are presented, which involves a Firebase installation identifier. It also uses Google Play to check for App updates and to show the rating prompt.

Subscriptions

Where a paid plan is offered, purchases and billing are handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The App stores what you have purchased on your device so it can unlock paid features. Google's privacy policy applies to your payment information.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • Android Auto Backup is enabled for the App, so its settings and last-scan summary may be included in your device's backup to your Google account, if you use that feature. Your recovered files are not part of the App's backup.
  • You can delete recovered files from within the App or using your device's file or gallery apps.
  • Uninstalling the App removes its private storage from your device. Files it recovered stay in their folders until you delete them.
  • Information collected by Google services is kept according to the retention settings of those services and Google's policies.
  • Emails you send us are kept for as long as needed to deal with your request. You can ask us to delete them.
