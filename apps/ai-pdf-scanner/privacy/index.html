Privacy Policy

AI PDF Scanner - Document Scanner — what stays on your device, and what leaves it.

Last updated 28 July 2026

Documents stay on your device

Files you import or scan are stored locally on your device and in your Downloads folder. They are not uploaded to our servers for storage.

What is sent for AI features

When you ask for a summary, use chat, or a document is categorised, the extracted text of that document is sent to our backend, which forwards it to our AI provider to generate a response. That text is processed for the request and is not retained.

Scanning and conversions

Scanned pages with no readable text layer, and files you convert between formats, are uploaded to our backend for processing and deleted once the conversion completes.

Permissions

The app asks for file access so it can find and open the documents already on your device, and for camera access when you scan a document. Scanned pages are processed on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
