Privacy Policy

QR Scanner & Barcode Scanner

Last updated 3 November 2026

At Sharkstack Developments we accept that privacy is important to all users. This policy applies to QR Scanner & Barcode Scanner for Android (the "App" or "Service") and informs users of our policies on the collection, use, and disclosure of information when they use it. For this App, it applies in place of our general privacy policy. The App is free to download and supported by advertising.

The App has no user accounts, and we do not run our own servers for it. If you use our Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. The information that we collect is used for providing and improving the Service. Our Subscription Terms apply to any paid plan.

Information Collection and Use

We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other registration details to use the App. The information described below is processed on your device or collected automatically by the Google services the App uses. We use it to run the App, show advertising, send notifications, and understand trends in how the App is used.

Your Codes and Images

Scanning and creating codes work on your device. The App does not upload your camera images, the photos you choose, or the contents of the codes you scan or create to us or to our analytics and advertising providers.

  • History and saved codes. Codes you scan, create, or save, along with their contents and any styling, are stored in the App's private storage on your device. Codes can contain sensitive information, such as Wi-Fi passwords or contact details. Android app backup is turned off for the App, so this data is not included in Android's backups of the App.
  • Settings. Your language and App preferences are stored on your device.
  • Images. An image you pick for scanning or as a logo is used only for that purpose. Logos and images you use while styling a code are kept on your device.
  • Clipboard. The App writes to your clipboard when you copy a result, and copies the Wi-Fi password when you choose to join a scanned network. The App does not read your clipboard.

Information leaves the App when you choose to send it elsewhere: for example, when you open a scanned link, share or export a code or your history, save an image to your gallery, or call or message a scanned contact. The app or website you use then handles it under its own privacy policy. Scanned links can lead to websites we do not control, so please check them before opening them.

Permissions

  • Camera. Used to read codes in the viewfinder. It is requested when you open the scanner, and no camera image is saved or transmitted. If you refuse, you can still scan from an image and create codes.
  • Photos. On current Android versions the system photo picker gives the App only the image you select. On Android 10 and older, the App may request storage access to read a selected image or save a code to your gallery.
  • Notifications. Used to show notifications on Android 13 and newer. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.
  • Internet and network state. Used for advertising, analytics, remote settings, and notifications. Scanning and creating codes work offline.
  • Vibration. Used for confirmation when a scan succeeds.
  • Advertising ID. Used as described under Advertising.

The App does not request your precise location, contacts, or microphone.

Information we obtain from third-party sources

Advertising partners. We may be provided with information about which ad network and advertising campaign an install of the App originated from, including through the Google Play install referrer. The App does not offer sign-in through social media accounts.

Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver, limit, and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process your device's Advertising ID, IP address, device and App information, and your interactions with ads. Ads may be personalised where permitted and where you have not opted out. Google's explanation of how it uses information from apps has more detail.

Where required, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App asks for your choices through Google's consent form. You can change them later under Settings > Ad privacy options, which appears when it applies to you. On Android you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. Choosing non-personalised ads does not remove ads; it changes how they are selected. If you tap an ad, the advertiser's own privacy policy applies to its site or app.

Log Data

Our App collects analytics data, through Google Analytics for Firebase, which we use to improve the App. The data contains contextual information about users, for example, coarse level location (country, city) derived from your IP address, device model, Android version, App version, language, app instance identifiers, and the dates and times of your use.

We also record how the App is used, such as screens viewed, the buttons and features used, whether a scan succeeded, the format and general type of a code (for example "URL" or "Wi-Fi"), and ad, permission, and rating events. These records do not include the contents of your codes or text you enter.

Where the consent form applies to you, analytics follows the choices you make in it. Elsewhere, analytics is on by default.

The App includes Firebase Crashlytics for error reporting, but its collection is currently turned off, so crash reports are not sent to us. We will update this policy before turning it on.

The barcode scanning component, Google ML Kit, processes codes on your device. It may send Google diagnostic and usage information, such as device and App information, performance metrics, error codes, and a per-installation identifier. Google describes this in its ML Kit data disclosure.

Notifications and Remote Settings

The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications, which creates a registration token for your installation of the App. It uses Firebase Remote Config to fetch settings that adjust App behaviour, such as how often ads appear, which involves a Firebase installation identifier. Neither includes the contents of your codes.

Subscriptions

Where a paid plan is offered, purchases and billing are handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The App may receive information from Google Play about what you have purchased so it can unlock paid features. Google's privacy policy applies to your payment information.

If you send feedback or contact us, the App opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. We receive what you choose to send, including your email address, your message, the App version, and any star rating you gave. We use it only to respond and improve the App.
